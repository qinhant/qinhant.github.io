I am a formal verification engineer at Apple. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Princeton University in 2025, advised by Prof. Sharad Malik. Before that, I completed my undergraduate studies at Zhejiang University.

My research interests include hardware formal verification, hardware security, and agentic formal verification tools. I use mathematical logic to rigorously reason about the functionality and security of systems.

My past work spans secure cache design, security verification of hardware enclaves, formal detection of transient execution vulnerabilities in processors, and functional subsetting of hardware accelerators.

Agentic Formal Verification (FV) Tools. I am interested in building AI agents that assist engineers throughout the formal verification workflow, from understanding RTL designs and writing properties to running verification tools, analyzing counterexamples, and refining proofs. My goal is to combine the flexibility of AI agents with the rigor of formal methods, making verification more accessible and efficient while grounding conclusions in results from formal verification tools.
